• Personalized Course Recommendations: Discover high-impact courses matched directly to your engineering branch, semester, and career goals.

• Institutional Student Directory: Filter students by academic year, branch, and VRSEC institutional domain (@vrsec.ac.in).
• Student Categorization: Identify high-performing students and at-risk students who need targeted pedagogical support.
• Course Assignment: Assign recommended courses directly to student cohorts with deadlines.
• Faculty AI Copilot: Generate quiz question banks, design semester syllabus outlines, and analyze student weakness patterns in seconds.

PRIVACY & SECURITY FIRST:

• Completely free of cost for all college students and instructors.

Transform the way you learn engineering. Download AI Personalized Learning today!
