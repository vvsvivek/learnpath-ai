## 1. Introduction

By downloading, accessing, or using the App, you consent to the data practices described in this document.

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Academic & User Information Provided by You:
- **Email Address:** Collected for authentication via secure One-Time Passcodes (OTP).
- **Full Name:** Used for dashboard personalization and institutional reports.
- **Academic Profile (Students):** Current academic year, engineering branch (e.g. CSE, IT, ECE), semester, CGPA, and selected learning interests.
- **Department & Designation (Faculty):** Used to verify instructional authority and cohort permissions.

### B. Automatically Collected Academic Progress Data:
- **Learning Path & Topic Completion:** Tracking which curriculum modules and topics you mark complete.
- **Study Session Timers:** Active time spent studying topics to compute learning velocity and total study hours.
- **Assessment & Quiz Performance:** Question response options, diagnostic scores, accuracy percentages, and detected knowledge gaps.
- **AI Tutor Interaction History:** Prompts and questions asked to the in-app AI assistant, stored securely to maintain conversational context.

### C. Device & Diagnostics Data:
- Operating system version, app performance logs, and error stack traces to diagnose platform reliability.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information
We use your information exclusively for educational and academic improvement purposes:
1. To dynamically generate personalized 4-phase learning paths and curated study resources using artificial intelligence.
2. To create adaptive diagnostic quizzes tailored to your semester and branch.
3. To provide faculty with aggregated departmental analytics on student comprehension and struggling topics.
4. To securely authenticate your account via email OTP without storing passwords.
5. To deliver platform health monitoring and prevent unauthorized abuse.

---

## 4. Third-Party Service Providers
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We interface only with the following service providers to deliver essential app functions:
- **Google Gemini API (Google AI):** Used to generate adaptive study paths and quiz banks based on academic prompts.
- **Supabase (PostgreSQL):** Secure cloud-hosted database storing user profiles, course data, and progress metrics with encrypted connections.
- **Brevo (Sendinblue):** Transactional email delivery service used exclusively for dispatching temporary 6-digit OTP verification codes.

---

## 5. Data Security & Storage
- All network communication between the mobile app, backend API, and database is encrypted using **Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS)**.
- User passwords are not collected; authentication uses cryptographic salted hashes of temporary OTPs that automatically expire within 5 minutes.
- Access tokens utilize industry-standard JSON Web Tokens (JWT) with automated token rotation.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy
Our application is designed specifically for higher education (B.Tech undergraduate students and faculty) aged 17 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
